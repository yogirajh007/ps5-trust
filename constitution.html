Nature of this document

This is a proposed private, good-faith members’ agreement. “Trust” is an honorary name. It does not claim to create a registered charitable trust, separate legal entity, or statutory trusteeship. The console remains the property of its owner.

Preamble

Whereas the Founding Member acquired a Sony PlayStation 5 gaming console and gifted it to Katniss, designated as Trustee;

Whereas the Trustee has permitted the possibility of shared enjoyment of the console;

Whereas the Members recognise that the release of Grand Theft Auto VI is an event of substantial cultural, recreational, and financial importance;

Whereas the Members desire an orderly understanding for voluntary fundraising, shared decision-making, and allocation of available gaming privileges;

Now therefore, the undersigned record this voluntary understanding, subject to the following Articles.

Article I — Establishment and Status

1.1. The association shall be known as The PlayStation 5 Trust, abbreviated as PS5T.

1.2. It is a voluntary, private, non-commercial association of its members.

1.3. “Trust” is an honorary designation. This document does not purport to create a registered charitable trust, separate legal entity, or statutory trusteeship.

1.4. The association continues until the Trustee withdraws permission to use the console or the members dissolve it under Article VIII.

Article II — Membership and Governance

2.1. Chairperson and Sole Trustee

Katniss, the console’s owner, shall serve as Chairperson and Sole Trustee. The Trustee retains full legal ownership and authority over the console’s location, safety, condition, and availability; may approve or reject Board resolutions affecting the console; and may veto any proposed activity affecting her property.

2.2. Board of Members

  • Founding Member and Voting Director: one (1) vote; may introduce motions, budgets, spending proposals, and gaming-allocation policies.
  • Rick, Non-Voting Member: zero (0) votes; may attend meetings, propose motions, present arguments, raise objections, participate in approved activities, and submit written dissent.

2.3. Decision-Making

A Board resolution passes with a simple majority of eligible votes cast. At present, this means one affirmative vote. Resolutions affecting the Trustee’s property or requiring pooled-fund expenditure also require the Trustee’s consent. Rick’s objections shall be recorded in the minutes.

Article III — The GTA VI Acquisition Fund

3.1. The members may establish a dedicated Grand Theft Auto VI Acquisition Fund.

3.2. The founding draft sets an indicative target of ₹5,999 for the Standard Edition. Confirm the current price before purchase.

3.3. Funds may come from voluntary member contributions, sponsorship, mutually agreed fundraising activities, or other lawful means approved by the Board and Trustee.

3.4. Contributions do not create ownership of the PS5 or game software, or an unconditional entitlement to use the console.

3.5. Contributions and expenses shall be recorded in a shared ledger with dates, amounts, and purposes.

3.6. Spending pooled funds requires Board approval and Trustee consent. Funds shall not be used for unrelated personal purchases.

3.7. Any digital game remains subject to its applicable licence and account terms.

Article IV — Gaming Access and Priority Credits

4.1. The members recognise that available gaming hours are limited and require an agreed allocation process.

4.2. The Trustee determines which hours are available for allocation.

4.3. Available time may be divided into sessions of two hours.

4.4. If demand exceeds supply, members may use non-monetary Priority Credits to express preferences through sealed bids.

4.5. The proposed initial rules are:

  • Each member receives 10 Priority Credits per week.
  • Members privately submit bids for published sessions.
  • The highest bidder receives priority for the selected session.
  • Winning credits are deducted; unsuccessful bids are returned.
  • Ties go to the member with fewer gaming hours in the preceding seven days.

4.6. Credits have no cash value and may not be bought, sold, or exchanged for money.

4.7. The Board may propose other credit rules, subject to Trustee approval.

4.8. No member may use the console contrary to the Trustee’s reasonable household requirements.

Article V — Trustee’s Reserved Authority

5.1. The Trustee may reserve gaming hours for personal use before publishing a schedule.

5.2. The Trustee may choose whether to participate in bidding.

5.3. The Trustee may suspend gaming where reasonably needed for household arrangements, equipment safety, or other reasonable considerations.

5.4. These privileges do not authorise personal use of pooled funds.

Article VI — Conflicts and Contributions

6.1. Members shall disclose a direct personal interest in proposed spending or allocation.

6.2. The Founding Member’s contribution toward the original console purchase is recognised as a gift, not an ownership interest or repayment obligation.

6.3. Contributions to the game fund are tracked separately from the original console purchase.

6.4. Members agree that gaming arrangements should not harm the relationships on which this association depends.

Article VII — Dispute Resolution

7.1. Members shall first try to resolve disputes through good-faith discussion.

7.2. Policy matters may be submitted to the Board.

7.3. The Trustee decides matters concerning the console, household arrangements, and physical access.

7.4. Rick may submit a written dissent for the permanent record.

Article VIII — Amendments and Dissolution

8.1. Amendments require a Board resolution and the Trustee’s written approval.

8.2. The association may be dissolved by unanimous agreement or when the Trustee withdraws permission to use the console.

8.3. On dissolution, the PS5 remains the Trustee’s property; unspent pooled funds shall be returned to contributors in proportion to their net contributions unless otherwise agreed; games remain subject to applicable purchase and licensing arrangements; and no member is entitled to compensation for emotional distress caused by losing access to Vice City.

Article IX — Ratification

The undersigned voluntarily adopt this Constitution as a record of their good-faith understanding.
