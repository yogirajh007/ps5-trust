1. Purpose and ownership

The Trust records an understanding between three members about the use of a PlayStation 5 and the purchase of games for shared enjoyment.

The console was given to Katniss as a gift and remains her property. She determines when it is available, approves activities affecting it, and may withdraw permission for shared use.

“Trust” is an honorary name. The charter is a proposed private members’ agreement and does not purport to create a registered charitable trust, separate legal entity, or statutory trusteeship.

A Board resolution passes with a majority of eligible votes cast, presently one affirmative vote. Any resolution affecting Katniss’s property or requiring pooled funds also needs her consent.

The Doctrine of Non-Contributory Representation

No member loses the right to express an opinion because they have not contributed money. The possession of an opinion does not confer a vote. Rick’s objections shall be preserved in the minutes.

3. Game acquisition fund

The Grand Theft Auto VI Acquisition Fund collects voluntary contributions toward the game’s purchase. The founding draft sets an indicative target of ₹5,999 for the Standard Edition; the current price must be confirmed before purchase.

  • Record all contributions and expenditure in a shared ledger.
  • Obtain Board approval and Katniss’s consent before spending pooled funds.
  • Keep contributions separate from the original console purchase.
  • Return unspent funds proportionately on dissolution, unless otherwise agreed.

A contribution creates no ownership interest in the console or game software and no unconditional right to play. Purchased games remain subject to their applicable licence and account terms.

4. Gaming access

Katniss decides which hours are available and may reserve time for her own use before publishing the schedule. Available time may be divided into two-hour sessions.

Where demand exceeds supply, the proposed charter uses sealed bids with non-monetary Priority Credits:

  1. Each member receives 10 credits per week.
  2. Members submit private bids for published sessions.
  3. The highest bidder receives priority, and their winning bid is deducted.
  4. Unsuccessful bidders retain their credits.
  5. Ties favour the member with fewer gaming hours in the preceding seven days.

Credits have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, or exchanged for money. Changes to these rules require Board approval and Katniss’s consent.

Example. If the Founder bids 8 credits, Rick bids 5, and Katniss bids 3, the Founder receives priority for the session and spends 8 credits. Rick and Katniss retain theirs. This is an illustration; the website does not run auctions or accept payments.

5. The founding constitution

The full constitution sets out the membership rules, fund administration, gaming arrangements, dispute process, amendment procedure, and provisions for dissolution.

Members should review and agree on its terms before signing. Katniss and Rick are named in the signature section; the Founder’s name and establishment date remain to be completed.
